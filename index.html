PET Fit

Makes the Productivity Entry Tool fit your phone screen.

Easiest: one link, no bookmark

Opens PET already fitted to your screen. Sign in as normal and you're done.

Open PET Fit

The first time, it asks you to paste your PET link once. After that, it opens PET straight away. Save it to your Favourites so it's one tap. If sign-in doesn't work there, use the bookmark below instead.

Or use the PET Fit bookmark

On a phone, the Productivity Entry Tool (Power Apps) squeezes itself into the narrow screen, so everything overlaps and the location box ends up off the edge. PET Fit is a bookmark. Tap it while PET is open and the page is laid out like on a laptop, then shrunk to fit your screen.

It only changes how the page is displayed, on your phone. It doesn't see or send your sign-in or any of your entries.

1

Copy the PET Fit code

If the button doesn't copy, press and hold in the box, then Select All and Copy.

2

Save it as a bookmark

  1. Bookmark this page: tap Share (or ⋯ then Share), then Add Bookmark. Name it PET Fit and tap Save.
  2. Open Bookmarks (the book icon), tap Edit, then tap PET Fit.
  3. Clear the address line and paste the code you copied. Tap Done.
  1. Bookmark this page: tap ⋯, then Add to Bookmarks.
  2. Tap ⋯ → Bookmarks, press and hold the new bookmark, and choose Edit Bookmark.
  3. Change the name to PET Fit, clear the URL and paste the code you copied. Tap Done.
  1. In Chrome, tap the ☆ star to bookmark this page.
  2. Tap Edit on the message that appears (or open Bookmarks and edit it).
  3. Name it PET Fit, clear the URL and paste the code you copied. Save.
3

Use it

  1. Open the Productivity Entry Tool as usual and sign in. If it asks, choose Continue in browser.
  2. Safari: open Bookmarks and tap PET Fit.
    Chrome: tap the address bar, type PET Fit and tap the bookmark that appears in the list (the one with the star).
  3. The page lays itself out properly and fits your screen.
📱Turn the phone sideways for bigger text, and pinch to zoom in on the part you need.
🔄Rotated the phone and it looks off? Tap PET Fit again.
↩️To go back to normal, just reload the page.
✅To check the bookmark works, tap it on this page. You'll get a message telling you to open PET first.

On a computer?

You don't need it there, but you can drag this button to your bookmarks bar: PET Fit

PET Fit isn't made by Microsoft or the PET team. It just changes how the page fits your screen.
